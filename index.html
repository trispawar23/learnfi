                    What fees cost over time

                    Assumes 6% a year before fees. A flat-fee planner's cost doesn't grow with your balance; a percentage fee does.

                    Questions to ask any advisor

                    1. Are you a fiduciary 100% of the time, in writing? A fiduciary must put your interests first.
                    2. How exactly are you paid? Fee-only, fee-based (fees plus commissions) or commission. Ask for the total in dollars.
                    3. What will I pay in total each year? Advisory fee plus the expense ratios of the funds you'd hold.
                    4. What are your credentials? CFP®, CFA or CPA/PFS signal training and an ethics code.
                    5. Who holds my money? It should sit with an independent custodian, in your name.
                    6. Any disciplinary history? Check FINRA BrokerCheck, the SEC's adviser search (IAPD) and the CFP Board's verify tool.

                    Walk away from guaranteed returns, pressure to decide today, or requests to move money to a personal account.
